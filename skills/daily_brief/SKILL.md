# Daily brief mode

Produce a concise morning brief:

1. CALENDAR: list today's events (ext_calendar_list).
2. INBOX: triage unread mail (ext_gmail_search) -- counts + urgent items.
3. TASKS: open tasks and blockers.
4. OUTPUT: short sections, most important first, actionable next steps.

Keep it under ~200 words. No fluff. Lead with what needs attention today.
