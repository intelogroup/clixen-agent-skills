# Worked examples — messaging judgment

Verdicts assigned by the skill's procedure (open the thread, read
in full, quote the deciding sentence). Cases 1–2 are the real
Level 7 inbox (2026-10-27); 3–5 are synthetic misleaders written
to defeat metadata-only triage.

## 1. Marc Joseph, BMC — "RE: Update" → loop_closed

Metadata says: human sender, reply thread, sounds open.
The thread: Jim emailed at 07:42 offering one day a week through
November. Marc's reply confirms the Monday return and states he
will follow up about late November himself.
Deciding sentence: his confirmation of the proposed schedule.
Verdict: **loop_closed**. No draft was created — deliberately.
A reply would have been noise on a settled thread.

## 2. Steven Keleti — "Re: New lease" → user_decision

The message offers a Dec 1 renewal at $2,200/mo. Nothing in it is
addressed wrongly; it simply asks for a decision only the user
can make about his own home and money.
Verdict: **user_decision**. Surfaced to Jim with the amount and
date. Never auto-answered, no draft acceptance or decline.

## 3. Synthetic — "RE: Invoice 1044" from a colleague → loop_closed

Body in full: "Got it, paid in full today — thanks!" The RE: and
the sender make it look like an open finance thread.
Deciding sentence: "paid in full today."
Verdict: **loop_closed**. Metadata-only triage would have drafted
a payment follow-up for a paid invoice.

## 4. Synthetic — landlord's agent, "Renewal terms" → user_decision

Body offers two renewal terms and asks "which works for you?"
The question is grammatically addressed to the reader — and it is
still not the agent's to answer: it commits the user's money and
home for a year.
Verdict: **user_decision**. Surface both terms verbatim.

## 5. Synthetic — group thread, user cc'd → not_mine

The open question ("Can you cover Saturday?") is addressed to
another named participant; the user is one of four cc's.
Verdict: **not_mine**. No draft, no nudge. Volunteering the user
for a shift he was never asked about is an unforgivable invention.
